# Excel Learning Resources

## Purpose

A practical, beginner-friendly collection of Excel learning workbooks, guided exercises, and reference materials for developing business analytics skills. Resources connect spreadsheet techniques to real-world accounting, sales, marketing, financial reporting, and business operations.

## Contents

- **Comprehensive Learning Workbooks**: Structured learning materials covering Excel shortcuts, data preparation, formulas, analysis, reporting, and visualization.
- **Guided Practice**: Realistic, clearly identified sample datasets, step-by-step exercises, and answer keys for independent learning.
- **Skill Development**: Formula practice, shortcut challenges, progress tracking, and repeatable analytical workflows.
- **Business Applications**: Examples of KPI calculation, performance analysis, dashboard design, and communicating findings.

Resources will be added as they are reviewed and ready to share.

## Documentation Standards

Each learning resource should identify:

1. **Learning Objective** – The skill or business question addressed.
2. **Prerequisites** – Required Excel version, knowledge, or supporting files.
3. **Instructions** – Clear, reproducible steps for learners.
4. **Practice Dataset** – Source, fields, and whether the data is fictional or real.
5. **Worked Examples** – Formula syntax, expected outputs, and explanations.
6. **Business Application** – How the skill supports practical decisions.
7. **Validation** – Checks for formulas, results, and data quality.
8. **Limitations** – Assumptions, version-specific features, and known restrictions.

## Contribution Notes

- Keep comprehensive educational workbooks here; place standalone formula references in [Excel Formula Library](../Excel-Formula-Library/) and reusable dashboards in [Dashboard Templates](../Dashboard-Templates/).
- Use descriptive file names and label fictional datasets clearly.
- Include only completed and verified work; do not present practice results as independently validated business findings.
- Do not upload confidential, proprietary, or personal information.
- Cite external data and sources where applicable.
